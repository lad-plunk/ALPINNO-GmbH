FROM INSIGHT TO DEVELOPMENT

A process for connecting evidence and imagination.

Based on Rowan’s Four Lenses of Innovation. All four lenses remain fully applicable; their implementation is redesigned to leverage big data and AI, moving from an analogue approach to a digital, AI-enabled workflow.

Four perspectives for discovering opportunities.

Challenging orthodoxiesQuestion established assumptions.

Harnessing trendsExplore emerging change.

Leveraging resourcesConsider new uses for skills and assets.

Understanding needsInvestigate unmet customer needs.

These are discovery perspectives, not sequential stages. The lenses remain the foundation; we redesign how the work is carried out, using relevant big data and AI to challenge the brief, connect evidence and triggers, develop ideas and support assessment.

THE DIGITAL, AI-ENABLED IMPLEMENTATION

AI-assisted, data-driven innovation.

AI helps organise and analyse evidence, suggest triggers, combine ideas and support assessment. People frame the challenge, check the evidence, test assumptions and decide what to develop.

  1. 01Frame the brief

    Clarify ambition, boundaries and assumptions.

  2. 02Select context

    Connect customer, market, technology and patent evidence.

  3. 03Explore domains

    Investigate different families of possible solutions.

  4. 04Find triggers

    Extract specific insights that can spark an invention.

  5. 05Generate ideas

    Combine triggers into alternative concepts.

  6. 06Assess and test

    Consider desirability, feasibility, viability and IP potential.

  7. 07Develop and capture

    Refine concepts, document inventions and plan development.

Learning can change the brief and send the work back to earlier steps. AI assists; human experts review the evidence, test assumptions and make decisions.

A FEW PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

Find the right starting point.

Who is the workshop for?+

Leadership, innovation, strategy and R&D teams looking for differentiated opportunities and a practical way to explore them. The challenge and working group are agreed during preparation.

Is this a software offer?+

The starting point is a consulting and capability-building engagement. We agree the appropriate tools, data access, deployment and any licensing requirements when scoping the work.

Does the workshop commit us to the pilot?+

Each stage supports a separate decision. The workshop helps clarify priorities and a potential pilot brief; pilot scope and the next investment are agreed afterwards.

Can our own team operate the approach?+

That is the ambition of Scale: train and coach internal facilitators, embed the appropriate process and tools, and connect the work to your organisation’s development and commercialisation decisions.
