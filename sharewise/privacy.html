ShareWise by ALPINNO

ShareWise privacy notice

Effective: 3 November 2026

Who is responsible and what this notice covers

This notice covers the planned Microsoft Store edition of ShareWise, including the base app and both subscription add-ons. Microsoft Store certification is pending; this notice does not announce Store availability. Portable-edition availability and licensing can be discussed with ALPINNO.

ShareWise is provided by ALPINNO GmbH, Acherhofstrasse 27, 6430 Schwyz, Switzerland. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to info@alpinno-solutions.ch or through our contact form.

Documents you choose to process

ShareWise accesses documents, images, pasted text, term lists and restoration files that you choose to open. These may contain personal or confidential information. It uses this content to extract text, perform printed-text OCR, identify and replace selected details, show results for review and restore matching placeholders when requested. Document analysis and OCR run on your computer using bundled models.

ShareWise does not send your documents, extracted text, restoration maps or learned terms to ALPINNO or an online AI service for processing. Sharing an exported result with another application or service is a separate action under your control and subject to that recipient's own practices.

Information kept on your computer

  • Preferences: language, interface and processing choices, output-folder locations and references to selected term-list files are stored in your Windows user profile. Preferences do not store copies of your source documents or the contents of imported term lists.
  • Correction learning: enabled by default, this remembers only exact terms and categories from your explicit keep/hide corrections. It does not learn from untouched suggestions. You can disable learning and separately clear saved corrections in Settings.
  • Saved outputs: exported text and restoration maps stay where you save them. The initial default is your actual Windows Desktop's ShareWise folder; you can choose another location.
  • Local diagnostic logs: optional and off by default in fresh installations. They record bounded technical metadata such as stages, counts, timings, resource use and safe error types. They exclude document contents, private values, source filenames/paths and licence contents. ShareWise does not automatically send these logs to ALPINNO. Logs rotate at a 1 MiB size threshold, with a current file and up to two backups; they have no fixed time-based expiry. Turning diagnostics off stops new logging but does not delete existing logs, which you can delete from the app's local state folder in your Windows user profile.
  • Temporary processing files: some operations create local temporary files. Temporary files are cleaned up during normal worker shutdown or cancellation. An interruption or system failure can leave residual files; secure erasure is not guaranteed.

Exported text, restoration maps and learned-term CSV files are not encrypted by ShareWise. Restoration maps contain original private values. Protect your Windows account, device and storage, and keep maps separate from text you share. If the Desktop or another selected folder is synchronized with OneDrive, backed up or shared, those services may copy files according to their own settings.

Copying output to the clipboard makes it available to Windows clipboard features and to applications you paste it into. ShareWise does not control those applications or your Windows clipboard settings.

Microsoft Store purchases and licences

The Store edition uses Microsoft Store services for purchases, subscriptions and licence checks. These operations can require Microsoft connectivity. The app uses Microsoft-issued licence status, subscription identifiers and expiry information to determine access to processing features. Valid cached licences may support offline processing while they remain valid. A Microsoft account may be needed for Store purchases and licensing; ShareWise does not require a separate ALPINNO account.

Microsoft handles the Store account, payment details, billing and cancellation. ShareWise does not collect your payment-card details or Microsoft account password. Microsoft services have their own privacy statement and terms; they do not replace this notice for ShareWise itself.

Microsoft reporting and app diagnostics

ShareWise has no ALPINNO usage-tracking or advertising service. Windows and Microsoft Store may separately process platform diagnostic and commerce information under Microsoft's settings and terms. Microsoft may make aggregate acquisition and sales statistics available to ALPINNO. These statistics are separate from Microsoft diagnostic and error reports. App analytics received from Microsoft are used only to improve ShareWise, not to identify individual users or devices or combine with third-party data.

ALPINNO does not currently access or download Microsoft diagnostic reports. If ALPINNO receives Microsoft diagnostic or error reports in future, it will use them only to investigate compatibility, bugs and quality issues. Such reports may include technical and personal information. Any personal information will be handled confidentially, with access restricted to authorised people investigating those issues, and deleted within 30 days of receipt or sooner if Microsoft requires. It will not be disclosed for other purposes.

Support and website contact

If you contact ALPINNO, it receives the information you choose to send, such as your contact details, issue description or attachments. Provide only what is needed and remove confidential material before sending it. We use this information to respond and provide support. Support enquiries are retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage any resulting business relationship and meet legal or record-keeping obligations. Unnecessary attachments containing personal or confidential information are deleted when no longer needed. Website/contact processing and privacy requests are described in the ALPINNO website privacy notice.

Retention and your controls

Saved outputs and restoration maps remain until you delete them. Preferences and learned corrections remain in your Windows user profile until you change, clear or delete them. Uninstalling the app is not a substitute for deleting exported files or copies in backups and synchronized folders.

Use Settings to change output locations, remove term-list references, disable or clear correction learning, and switch local diagnostics on or off. Disabling learning does not itself delete earlier saved corrections; clearing is a separate action. Deleting a term-list reference does not delete the source file.

You can access or delete saved outputs and maps in their Windows folders. Previously saved files are not deleted when a licence expires. Clearing corrections does not remove earlier exports or maps. For personal information held by ALPINNO through support or reporting, contact us using the details above. Microsoft account and billing privacy requests should be directed to Microsoft.

Privacy requests and changes

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal information held by ALPINNO, object to processing, or withdraw consent for future processing. Contact us using the details above; identity verification may be needed. Direct Microsoft account and billing privacy requests to Microsoft.

We update this notice when relevant ShareWise features or data practices change. The current notice and its effective or updated date are published on this page.
