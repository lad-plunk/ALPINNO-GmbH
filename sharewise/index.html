1. ShareWise and ChatGPT: the workflow

Before you send a document to ChatGPT, prepare it with ShareWise. Hide detected private details locally, then share the prepared text with ChatGPT or another AI. If you need the original names back in the answer, restore them locally in ShareWise. That final step is optional. You choose what to share.

2. Entirely on your computer

ShareWise runs entirely on your computer, with no internet connection required. Even completely disconnected, you can prepare documents and restore private details. Your document content is not sent to a server for processing. Choose a Word document, a text-based PDF or a PowerPoint file. Detected private details become consistent placeholders. The detection model achieved 98% token-level recall in benchmark testing. Results vary by document.

3. Your confidential-term lists

And you can make it specific to your business. Add a CSV list of client names, companies or project references you want hidden. ShareWise automatically replaces exact matches in the extracted text, alongside its AI detection. Your own confidential-term lists add another layer of control.

4. Two ways to work

ShareWise gives you two ways to work: Minimal mode for one-click preparation, and Advanced mode for review and control.

5. Minimal: select a file

In Minimal mode, select your file and ShareWise does the rest: prepares the text, saves the result and opens the output folder. One simple step to remove detected private details before sharing. Even when you're short on time, privacy preparation can be part of your routine.

6. Advanced: review and adjust

For more sensitive documents, use Advanced mode to give the result your final approval. An intuitive side-by-side view aligns the original extracted text with the redacted version, making it easy to check the automatic work and adjust what stays or goes. Review the unhighlighted text too, then share the prepared result with greater confidence.

7. Your decisions, remembered

ShareWise also remembers your explicit keep and hide decisions locally. When those same terms appear again, your saved choices are applied, helping you avoid repeating the same corrections.

8. Optional local restoration

Now take the prepared text into ChatGPT or another AI. Need the original names back in the answer? Bring the response into ShareWise and restore matching placeholders using your private restoration file. Keep that file to yourself.

9. An efficiency benefit

There's an efficiency benefit too. In our illustrative image-rich PDF comparison, leaving out unnecessary visuals reduced document input tokens by about 90%. Actual savings depend on the document and how the AI processes it.

Practical document support

Work with the text in files you already use.

ShareWise is a portable Windows x64 application. It prepares extracted text from common files or text you paste into the app.

  • DOCX and PPTX
  • Text-based PDF
  • TXT and Markdown
  • Pasted text

Files can be up to 25 MB, with a limit of 200 PDF pages per file. Prepared text can be saved as TXT or Markdown; restored text can also be saved as RTF.

Clear boundaries

You make the final decision.

  • Scanned or image-only files need OCR elsewhere.
  • XLSX is not supported.
  • ShareWise prepares extracted text. It does not change or redact the original formatted document.

Some images, charts and complex layout elements do not yield text. Check the extracted text and all unhighlighted content before sharing. ShareWise cannot guarantee that every private detail is found.

Privacy on your terms

Your document is not sent to ShareWise servers.

ShareWise prepares and restores text on your computer. It has no required cloud account, telemetry, automatic model download or online processing step. You choose whether to take reviewed text to ChatGPT or another AI service.

That external AI step has its own data handling terms. Review the complete prepared text and your chosen service's settings before sharing.

Keep private support files private.

The optional restoration file contains original values and is not encrypted. Never upload it with the prepared text. Optional correction memory stores your chosen terms locally, also without encryption; you can disable or clear it in Settings.

Common questions

A clear fit for your workflow.

ShareWise is a focused preparation tool. The AI service remains your choice, and your review remains part of the process.

Can I use ShareWise completely offline?

Yes. Document preparation and optional restoration work on a disconnected Windows computer. Using ChatGPT or another online AI service is a separate step you choose to take.

Does ShareWise upload my document to ChatGPT?

No. ShareWise does not automatically send your document or prepared text to an AI service. You review the output and decide what to share.

Will it find every private detail?

No detection system can promise that. Review the entire extracted and prepared text, including content that was not highlighted. The 98% figure above describes the underlying model on a published benchmark, not the result for your document.
