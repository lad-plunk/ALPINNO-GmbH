Where ALPINNO can help

Consulting, training and implementation.

ALPINNO combines AI and digital capabilities, innovation and IP expertise, and Agile execution methods to help organisations move from strategic opportunity to implemented value.

01

AI Transformation and Capability Building

Help leaders and teams define an AI strategy, integrate it into the wider digital-transformation roadmap and build the skills, governance, workflows and tools required to create practical business value.

  • AI strategy and integration into digital-transformation roadmaps
  • Discovery, assessment and prioritisation of AI opportunities and use cases
  • Workflow, automation and AI-agent design
  • AI governance, risk, standards and regulatory readiness
  • AI-literacy programmes supporting obligations such as Article 4 of the EU AI Act
  • Hands-on implementation, adoption and change enablement
  • Leadership and team workshops
  • Prompt engineering and effective use of leading AI tools
  • Role-specific AI training and one-to-one coaching
  • Advanced and agentic ways of working
02

AI-Assisted Data-Driven Innovation and IP

Combine proprietary and public data, structured innovation methods and AI to discover opportunities, create differentiated concepts and capture protectable inventions throughout the front end of innovation and the new-product-development process.

  • Innovation blueprints, briefs and opportunity framing
  • Customer, market, company, patent, scientific and technology intelligence
  • AI-assisted data-driven ideation and cross-pollination
  • Concept generation, development, evaluation and prioritisation
  • Invention capture and invention-disclosure creation throughout NPD
  • AI-assisted preliminary prior-art and patentability analyses
  • AI-assisted preliminary freedom-to-operate screening
  • Patent landscapes and technology landscapes
  • Innovation, invention and IP-process design and improvement

03

Agile Execution, Projects, Programmes and PMO Excellence

Strengthen and scale the methods, governance, tools and working practices used to deliver innovation and new-product development with speed, transparency and appropriate control.

  • Agile methods, including Scrum, Kanban and Agile at Scale
  • Hybrid NPD methods, including Agile stage-and-gate approaches
  • Agile, hybrid and waterfall NPD-process design and improvement
  • Project, programme, risk and project-portfolio management
  • PMO processes, portfolio governance, tools and reporting systems
  • Selection, development and implementation of digital project and portfolio tools
  • Process documentation, procedures and standard operating procedures
  • Team workshops, training, facilitation and review meetings
